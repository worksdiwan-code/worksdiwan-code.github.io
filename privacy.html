Privacy policy

Effective 12 October 2026. This policy covers Diwan Office, the private desktop app used by Diwan Studio, and this website.

Who uses the app

Diwan Office is used by one person: the owner of Diwan Studio, on their own computer. It is not distributed, sold or offered to other users. It only connects to accounts that belong to Diwan Studio.

What the app accesses

  • Pinterest: the studio's own Pins, boards and Pin analytics; creating Pins on the studio's own boards; and public keyword trend data. It does not read other people's Pins, boards or personal information.
  • Etsy: the studio's own shop, listings and orders (after the owner signs in), and public listing data used for market research. For an order, the app reads the buyer's name, shipping address and any personalisation text they typed.
  • Printify: the studio's own shops and products, to prepare product drafts.
  • Fiverr clients: briefs and photos that clients send for their thumbnail order, pasted into the app by the owner.

Where data is kept

Everything the app collects is stored only on the owner's computer. Access tokens are kept in the operating system's secure credential store (the macOS Keychain), never in files. The app has no server and no user accounts, and it does not use advertising, tracking or analytics tools.

Sharing

Diwan Studio does not sell, rent or share data from Pinterest, Etsy, Printify or clients with anyone. To create designs, the app sends design instructions and order briefs to AI providers (Anthropic and OpenAI) under their API terms; both state that API data is not used to train their models by default. Data from Pinterest is never sent to these providers in a way that identifies other people. Client photos have their background removed on the owner's computer and are not uploaded for that step.

For an Etsy order, the buyer's name and shipping address are sent to Printify only to make and deliver that order, when the owner sends it; for a personalised item, the personalisation text the buyer typed (for example a name and a date) is sent to Anthropic so it can be lettered correctly. Tracking numbers from Printify are given back to Etsy so the buyer is told their order shipped.

Keeping and deleting data

Cached data from Pinterest, Etsy and Printify is refreshed regularly and deleted when no longer needed. You can remove the app's access to a Pinterest account at any time in your Pinterest settings, in the list of connected apps. To ask about or delete any data relating to you, email the contact address below; requests are answered within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
